Cold · fasted · AM · pre-shower. Never after training, never after a shower — hydrated skin reads thick.

NECK IS PEGGED AT 18.00 — measure below the larynx or not at all. Anything above the larynx reads 18.75 and is a trap artefact.

Rotate 1→6, then repeat ×3. Never three in a row at one site — the pinch pushes fluid and readings drift down.

Photograph the marks. Re-mark from last month's photo, not from memory.

Red = out of range. That means a typo, a decimal slip, or mm entered instead of inches. Re-read before you send it.
